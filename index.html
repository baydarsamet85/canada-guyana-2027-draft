Questions

FAQ & contact

Who can apply?+

Canadian women who own or lead businesses, senior corporate and not-for-profit leaders, and young women entering international business. B7

What does it cost?+

CAD $2,500 plus taxes, covering meetings, the program, hosted meals and the briefing. Flights, hotel and insurance are arranged separately. B2

How are meetings arranged?+

After acceptance you tell us who you want to meet. We arrange meetings with the High Commission of Canada and Guyanese chambers, sector bodies and industry organizations.

Do I need a visa?+

It depends on your citizenship. We cover entry requirements in the briefing. See the Government of Canada’s travel advice for Guyana. B15

How is safety handled?+

A Duty of Care Lead manages travel safety and emergency protocols. Travel insurance is required, and all delegates agree to a code of conduct. B15

What if I need to cancel?+

Refunds depend on timing, and you can transfer your seat to a qualified colleague. See the policy on the Delegates page. A7
